Scrambled Eggs

Egg are of my favourite foods.Here is a recipe for deliciously rich scrambled egg .

Ingredients

  • 2 Eggs
  • 1tbs butter
  • 2tb cream

Method

  1. Melt butter in a frying pan over a medium heat
  2. Gently mix the eggs and cream in a bowl
  3. Once butter has melte add cream and eggs
  4. Using a spatula fold the eggs from the eedge of the pan to the center every 20 second (as if you are making an omelette)
  5. When the eggs are still moist remove from the heat ( it will continue to cook on the plate until served )
